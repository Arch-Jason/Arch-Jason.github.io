Hi, I’m Arch-Jason. I’m going to study Electrical Engineering at UofT, and I’m really interested in building things and understanding how things work from the ground up.

I’ve always enjoyed DIY projects, especially ones that combine hardware and software. I like taking an idea, designing the hardware, writing the firmware, and making the whole system actually work.

Before university, I worked on a lot of embedded and electronics projects. I’ve designed PCBs and built systems with microcontrollers like ESP32 and STM32, worked with communication protocols like CAN and UART, and experimented with software-defined radio and digital signal processing. I also built some projects involving FPGA and RF systems, because I’m really interested in how signals are processed and transmitted.

Besides engineering, I also enjoy photography and building tools for my own interests. For example, I created an online platform for railway photography enthusiasts, which was a fun combination of software development and solving real-world problems.

For me, engineering is mostly about curiosity and problem solving. I enjoy learning something new, taking things apart, and building something that didn’t exist before. I’m looking forward to meeting people here and hopefully working on some interesting projects together.
